How the numbers work. Blended rate is the balance-weighted average of the two note rates. Cash-flow rate is the monthly yield that equates combined loan proceeds with every scheduled payment on both liens, so it reflects each loan's term. Payment-equivalent rate is the rate a single new loan of the same term would need to produce the same starting P&I on the combined balance.

Modeling assumptions. MIP/MI on the assumed loan is charged monthly on the current balance for the whole period shown. PMI on the new loan drops when the scheduled balance reaches 78% of the purchase price. Prepaids, escrow reserves, lender credits and seller concessions are excluded. Recording, title and transfer taxes are the same for both structures and are left out of cash to close.

Estimates only. Listing-portal rate and balance figures are estimates. Get a payoff/assumption statement from the servicer and a written second-lien quote before relying on these results.
